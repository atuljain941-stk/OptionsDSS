🎯 First Pullback Scanner — Sharp Move Retracement
Finds stocks like QCOM/AMD that had a sharp move (≥ move%), then pulling back for the first time (3-8 days) with trend still intact. The last X days must resume the trend direction or stay sideways. Both overbought and oversold scanned.
🌡️ Trend Exhaustion → Second Pullback
Looks back X bars/periods for a prior RSI exhaustion condition (RSI high/low plus RSI-EMA90 stretch), then finds the names that already bounced once and are now on the second pullback. Use a higher timeframe for exhaustion and a lower timeframe for the entry trigger. For oversold exhaustion, the trade bias is often calls; for overbought exhaustion, it is often puts. Oversold setups require RSI ≤ the low threshold and RSI-EMA90 ≤ -the diff threshold.
Symbol optional — leave blank to scan all watchlist symbols. Expiry optional — blank auto-picks best in Min/Max DTE range. Select a Sector to narrow to that sector only. Grade: A≥65 · B≥45 · C<45
Signal logic:  RSI-14 − EMA90(RSI) ≥ +20 = OVERBOUGHT → avoid long calls, prefer bear spreads or IC.  RSI-14 − EMA90(RSI) ≤ −20 = OVERSOLD → avoid long puts, prefer bull spreads.  OI walls used as strike anchors. ATR sizes the wings. PoP estimated from distance-to-strike.
