🦅 Iron Condor / Vertical Scanner

Scans the whole watchlist for a chosen expiry, finds symbols with a put wall below spot and a call wall above it (condors), or a single strong wall near spot (bull put / bear call verticals) — graded by wall strength, distance, PCR, and IV percentile proxy, plus a recent 5-day price-action flag when the trend is running toward the wall the setup depends on. Each wall also shows a freshness status (● fresh / ● flat / ● unwinding) based on real OI change, since a wall's raw size says nothing about whether that positioning was built recently or is old and unrevisited.
