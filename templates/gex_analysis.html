Net Gamma

Call gamma less put gamma. Positive net gamma can encourage dealer hedging that dampens moves; negative net gamma can amplify directional moves. It is a regime context—not a stand-alone trade signal.

Absolute Gamma

Call plus put gamma. This shows where the total hedging sensitivity is concentrated, regardless of direction. The largest strike is a useful pin/reference level, especially close to expiration.

OI Change

Change versus the previous saved snapshot. It is not a live OI feed: published open interest is typically updated once per session. Positive change is blue; negative change is red.
