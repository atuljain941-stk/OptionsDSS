Net Gamma

Call gamma less put gamma. Positive net gamma can encourage dealer hedging that dampens moves; negative net gamma can amplify directional moves. It is a regime context—not a stand-alone trade signal.

Absolute Gamma

Call plus put gamma. This shows where the total hedging sensitivity is concentrated, regardless of direction. The largest strike is a useful pin/reference level, especially close to expiration.

Call vs Put Gamma

Shows composition at every strike: calls above zero and puts below zero. Compare the largest call and put concentrations with spot to frame possible overhead/responsive levels.

How to use it: The attached transcript describes using the maximum-gamma strike as the reference for a neutral 0DTE iron-fly concept. This page exposes that concentration, but does not make a trade recommendation. Verify live price, liquidity, expiry risk, and your own risk limits before acting.
