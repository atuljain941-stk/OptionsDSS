How the journal works
Outlook is derived from the live price versus the spread strikes. PNR is computed from the PNR method formula using long strike, DTE, and ATR. The action engine prioritizes profit-taking at 94% of max value, 50% loss orders after PNR breach, and early exits as DTE contracts.
