Auto Trading - Schwab EOD Stop/OCO
End-of-day workflow for the EMA5 body-breakdown short strategy. The app scans completed daily candles, builds next-session broker stop-entry orders, and uses Schwab TRIGGER + OCO children so Schwab monitors the entry and exits. This avoids realtime quote load and app-side trigger delay.
