EMA, MACD, ADX / DMI, RSI, and squeeze-release confirmations are all tied to the controls above. Strictness changes the minimum confirmations and score floor.
Bias and trade type
Bias can filter bullish or bearish setups. Trade type can limit the scan to call spreads, put spreads, or either.
Risk logic
PNR = long strike - (long strike × DTE × ATR) / 2000. Width is still 1 or 5, and the expiry defaults to the nearest 28–45 DTE.
