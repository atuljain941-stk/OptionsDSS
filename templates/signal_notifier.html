▾Backtest — options-trade alerts vs actual outcome ⬇ Export CSV
For every PS/CS/IC alert whose expiry has already passed, checks the underlying's actual closing price near expiry (from your local price cache — no live fetch) and computes what the credit spread would have earned held to expiry with no early management. Compares the realized win rate to the scanner's own predicted POP, so you can see if its probability estimates are actually calibrated. This is a worst-case-timing baseline, not a replay of your real Trade Management exits (which usually close well before expiry).
