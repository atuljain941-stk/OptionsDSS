📐 Swing Positioning Scanner

The multi-day version of the 0DTE/GEX work — reads stock volume vs price over the last ~2 weeks (Wyckoff effort-vs-result: heavy volume with little price movement is absorption or distribution, a leading indicator, not noise) and crosses it against options walls at a 20-45 DTE swing horizon. Only flags a symbol when the stock-side signal and the options-side signal agree — either one alone is a hypothesis, not a high-probability setup.
