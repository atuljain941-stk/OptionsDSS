Task Pool Health
Two separate pools on purpose: the Interactive pool is for scans someone is actively waiting on; the Background pool is for watchlist Fetch Price/OI, the 7:30 AM sweep, and other bulk work nobody's watching in real time. Kept apart so a background fetch can never starve an interactive scan of workers (or vice versa) — that contention used to cause scans to time out at 100% for no visible reason whenever a bulk fetch happened to be running at the same time.
