🎯 Greeks Strategy Scanner

Multi-leg strategies (short strangle, short straddle, iron fly) sized and priced from real Black-Scholes Greeks and stored option premiums, filtered by RR (max profit / max loss, from the actual expiry payoff curve) and POP (probability of profit — lognormal breakeven probability, the same convention Black-Scholes itself and most retail POP tools use, not a delta shortcut). Defined-risk structures pass a real RR floor far more often than undefined-risk ones — that's the math working correctly, not a filter to loosen.
