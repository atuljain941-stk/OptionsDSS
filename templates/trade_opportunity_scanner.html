How the Scanner Works

Entry Quality Score (0–100)

Six factors are weighted to produce a single 0–100 score:

Factor Weight What it measures
Regime Alignment25 ptsIs the regime (bullish/bearish/sideways) aligned with the trade direction?
RS vs SPY20 ptsIs the stock outperforming (for bulls) or underperforming (for bears)?
IV Rank15 ptsCredit trades want high IVR (sell expensive premium). Debit wants low IVR.
PCR15 ptsPut/Call Ratio — bullish flow supports bull trades, bearish flow supports bear trades.
OI Walls15 ptsPut wall (support) for bulls. Call wall (resistance) for bears. Walls near spot = more conviction.
Gamma Flip10 ptsAbove gamma flip = dealers stabilising (supports bulls). Below = trend amplification (supports bears).

Grades

A ≥80 — OPEN B ≥65 — OPEN C ≥50 — OPEN SMALL D ≥35 — OPEN SMALL F <35 — AVOID

Trade Selection Logic

  • Bull Put Spread (PS) — UPTREND or RSI-EMA diff > −5. Sell put below spot, buy further put. Collect credit. Wins if stock stays above sell strike.
  • Bear Call Spread (CS) — DOWNTREND or RSI-EMA diff < 5. Sell call above spot, buy further call. Wins if stock stays below sell strike.
  • Iron Condor (IC) — SIDEWAYS trend, IVR ≥35. Sell put spread below AND call spread above. Wins if stock stays in the profit zone.

DTE Window (7–30 days)

Preferred range: 14–21 DTE (Fridays preferred). This is the theta sweet spot — enough time value to collect meaningful credit, but short enough that theta decay is working hard. Below 7 DTE = gamma risk. Above 30 DTE = theta works too slowly.

Earnings Guard

Any symbol with earnings within the guard window (default 14 days) is automatically excluded. This prevents entering positions that could be blown up by a binary earnings event.

PNR (Price Not Right)

Calculated as: long_strike − (long_strike × DTE × ATR) / 2000. This is your price-based stop level. If spot closes beyond PNR, place a 50% loss order per the PNR Method.
