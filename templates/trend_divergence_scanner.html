📐 Trend + Divergence Scanner

Scans a watchlist on one timeframe for TB (bull trend flip), TS (bear trend flip), and classic/hidden bull & bear divergence. Each match includes entry, stop (tight single-candle, ATR fallback), target, and whether it hit target/stop/still open based on price since the signal.

Scan Parameters

Only applies to Classic/Hidden Divergence conditions -- filters for divergences where RSIDiff90 hit ±threshold at some point in the lookback window before the pivot (a real prior move that exhausted, not just chop). Unchecking "Match reversal direction" accepts an extreme on either side.
⚠ 5m/15m have no local price cache -- this scan will hit the live data provider once per symbol and may be slow, capped at ~59 days of history.

Backtest Parameters

⚠ Measured throughput: roughly 0.07 sec per (bar × symbol × timeframe). A 10-symbol watchlist over 2 timeframes and ~1 year of history takes on the order of 20-25 minutes. Start with a small watchlist and a short date range to get a feel for it before running your full list. This runs in the background -- the page polls status, you don't have to keep it open the whole time, but closing/reloading loses the live progress display (the run itself keeps going server-side; reload and check "Recent Runs" below to resume watching it).
⚠ 5m/15m have no local cache — including them means a live fetch per symbol on top of whatever cached timeframes you pick.
