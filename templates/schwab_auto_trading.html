Open Positions

A background job polls combined P&L for every OPEN position every 30 seconds and auto-closes on target/stop-loss hit using the same safe sequencing as a manual close.

Pending Orders (live from Schwab)

Every order still working at the broker -- GTC limits sitting unfilled, queued/pending-activation orders, anything not yet filled/canceled/rejected. A background job checks these every minute; when one fills, the matching position flips to OPEN and gets logged to the Journal automatically.
